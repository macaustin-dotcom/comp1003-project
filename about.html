THE BEGINNING

A DIFFERENT KIND OF COMFORT.

The story of Dr. Martens began in 1945, when German doctor Klaus Martens created an air-cushioned sole while recovering from a foot injury.

Working with engineer Dr. Herbert Funck, he developed the footwear designed to offer a different kind of comfort from traditional hard sole shoes.

Their invention would eventually become the foundation of one of the most recognizable boot designs in the world.

OUR JOURNEY

FROM WORK BOOT TO ICON

A few important moments that shaped the Dr. Martens story.

1945

THE IDEA

Klaus Martens develops an air-cushioned sole designed to provide greater comfort.

1959

THE UK CONNECTION

The Griggs family acquires the rights to manufacture the footwear in Britain and develops the distinctive boot design.

1960

THE 1460 IS BORN

On April 1, the eight-eyelet 1460 boot arrives, named after its production date.

1961

THE 1461

The three-eyelet 1461 shoe follows, bringing the same distinctive design to a different style of footwear.

1970's

A CULTURAL ICON

Musician and youth subcultures embrace the boots, transforming them into a symbol of identity and self-expression.

THE SPIRIT OF DOCS

STAND OUT.
BE YOURSELF.

Dr. Martens became more than footwear. From music and street culture to personal style, the boots became a way for people to express who they are.

The yellow stitching, grooved sole and distinctive heel loop remained recognizable details of our design that has stood the test of time.
