THE BEGINNING

A DIFFERENT KIND OF COMFORT.

The story of Dr. Martens began in 1945, when German doctor Klaus Martens created an air-cushioned sole while recovering from a foot injury.

Working with engineer Dr. Herbert Funck, he developed the footwear designed to offer a different kind of comfort from traditional hard sole shoes.

Their invention would eventually become the foundation of one of the most recognizable boot designs in the world.
